# Privacy Policy

**Infinity Wash**
Version 1.0 · Effective October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Infinity Wash ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the Infinity Wash mobile app. It is written in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules. By creating an account, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described here.

## 1. Who we are

We operate a laundry pickup and delivery service in the Philippines and control the personal information processed through this app.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account details** — your first and last name, mobile number, email address and password.

**Delivery details** — your primary address, a landmark to help riders find you, and, if you choose to drop one, a GPS pin of your delivery location.

**Order information** — the services you request, pick-up and delivery times, the recorded weight of your laundry, add-ons, order status history and rider assignment.

**Photos of your laundry** — taken at check-in so both of us have a record of the condition and quantity of the items we received.

**Payment information** — the payment method, the amount, your GCash reference number and, where you upload one, your GCash payment receipt. We do **not** collect or store your GCash password, MPIN or other credentials.

**Communications** — messages you exchange with staff and riders through the app's chat, plus your feedback and ratings.

**Device and technical data** — your device's push notification token so we can deliver order updates, and diagnostic information needed to keep the app working.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information such as government-issued ID numbers, health data, or religious or political beliefs. Please do not enter such information into the app.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- create and manage your account and authenticate you;
- schedule, route and complete pickups and deliveries;
- calculate charges, process payments and issue receipts;
- send you order updates and service notifications;
- respond to your questions, feedback and claims;
- keep records required by law, and to investigate lost or damaged items, payment disputes and misuse;
- improve our service, routes and reliability using aggregated figures that do not identify you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Location data

The app asks for location permission so you can drop a delivery pin and so riders can navigate to you and share progress. Your chosen pin is stored on your profile and attached to your orders.

Riders' live locations are visible to us and, for an order in progress, to the customer receiving it. You can refuse location permission and type your address instead; some features, such as live tracking, will then be unavailable.

## 5. Photos of your laundry

Photos taken at check-in are attached to your order. We use them as the shared record of what was received, to resolve claims about quantity or condition, and to confirm delivery. They are stored with your order and are visible to the staff and rider handling it.

## 6. Payment information

GCash payments are settled through GCash itself; we only receive the confirmation and the reference number you supply or that our verification records produce. Receipt images you upload are stored in secure storage and used to verify the payment.

## 7. When we share your information

We share the minimum needed to run the service:

- **Riders and staff** — your name, delivery address, landmark, pin and contact number, so the right person can be reached for the right order.
- **Service providers** — Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Google Firebase (push notifications) and Google Maps or OpenStreetMap providers (maps and routing). They process data on our instructions.
- **GCash / payment processors** — to the extent needed to confirm a payment you made.
- **Authorities** — where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

We never share your information for anyone else's marketing.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed afterwards to meet tax, accounting and legal record-keeping requirements, and to handle any claim. Order and payment records are generally retained for the period required by Philippine law. When data is no longer needed it is deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information sooner — see Section 10 — though we may retain what the law requires us to keep.

## 9. How we protect it

We protect your information using encrypted transport in transit, database row-level security that restricts each user to their own records, encrypted local storage for sign-in tokens on your device, and access limited to staff who need it for their role. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

## 10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may:

- **be informed** — ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
- **access a copy** of that information;
- **rectify** anything that is inaccurate or out of date;
- **object** to processing, including direct communication;
- **block or erase** information we no longer need;
- **be indemnified** for damages from unauthorised processing;
- **file a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time. We will stop the processing that relied on it, and tell you which services we can no longer provide as a result.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We respond within the period required by law. Withdrawing consent, or asking us to delete your data, means we may have to close your account, because we cannot provide pickup and delivery without your delivery details.

## 11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without parental consent, contact us and we will remove it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing or the law changes. The version and effective date at the top tell you which version applies. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect, and we may ask you to confirm acceptance of a new version.
